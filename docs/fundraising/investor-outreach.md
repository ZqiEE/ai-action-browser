# AI Browser Investor Outreach Pack

Use this for targeted outreach to investors with relevant experience in browsers, consumer AI, search, web infrastructure, commerce, advertising, developer platforms, or marketplaces.

Do not send a mass campaign. Personalize the first paragraph and explain why the recipient is relevant.

The positioning hierarchy must remain:

1. AI browser;
2. user-controlled completion of web tasks;
3. U.S. laptop shopping as the first validation wedge;
4. outcome-funded commercial model.

Do not lead with “shopping assistant,” “consumer commerce product,” “affiliate platform,” or “results marketplace.”

## Target-list fields

Track these fields for every contact:

- name;
- firm;
- role;
- relevant browser, consumer AI, search, commerce, infrastructure, or marketplace experience;
- why this person is a fit;
- warm introduction path;
- date contacted;
- response;
- requested evidence;
- next follow-up date;
- status: research, warm, contacted, meeting, pass, active diligence.

## Outreach rules

- send 5–10 highly relevant messages per week during validation;
- always identify the product first as an AI browser;
- state that shopping is the first validation wedge, not the product category;
- describe outcome revenue as the business model, not as the user-facing product;
- do not claim traction that does not exist;
- do not claim current live AI, cross-site execution, payment, merchant settlement, or advertising systems when using demo data;
- lead with the browser product and the economic question, not API expenses;
- ask for feedback before asking for money during the preheating period;
- convert to a formal raise only when the demo, task-cost data, user evidence, and a credible commercial path are ready.
