## Target-list fields

Track these fields for every contact:

- name;
- firm;
- role;
- relevant investments or operating experience;
- why this person is a fit;
- warm introduction path;
- date contacted;
- response;
- requested evidence;
- next follow-up date;
- status: research, warm, contacted, meeting, pass, active diligence.

## Outreach rules

- send 5–10 highly relevant messages per week during validation;
- do not claim traction that does not exist;
- do not claim current live AI, payment, merchant, or advertising systems when using demo data;
- lead with the product and the economic question, not API expenses;
- ask for feedback before asking for money during the preheating period;
- convert to a formal raise only when the demo, task-cost data, and user evidence are ready.
