## Trust and browser questions

Ask after the participant completes the flow:

1. What kind of product did you think this was?
2. What is the difference between Search, Compare, and Prepare?
3. What did the browser recommend and why?
4. What information was uncertain or potentially out of date?
5. Which content, if any, was paid or Sponsored?
6. What would happen after pressing Prepare?
7. What important action would still require your confirmation?
8. Did any real payment or purchase occur?
9. Would you use this browser workflow instead of starting with a conventional search engine for a similar task? Why?
10. What other web task would you expect this browser to handle?

## Investor-ready summary

Report only measured results and clearly label modeled commercial assumptions:

- number of unique participants;
- number of completed and abandoned sessions;
- percentage who understood the product as an AI browser;
- Search, Compare, and Prepare comprehension rates;
- completion and fallback rates;
- recommendation-comprehension rate;
- Sponsored-identification rate;
- Prepare rate and merchant-handoff rate;
- ordinary-search preference rate;
- stated repeat-use intent for the browser workflow;
- average and p95 total task cost;
- average model calls per task;
- cache and unchanged-evidence reuse rate;
- retry and failure spend;
- strongest commercial outcome signal;
- expected and received commercial value per 100 tasks;
- estimated gross contribution per 100 tasks;
- main reason users abandoned, distrusted, or misunderstood the browser flow.
