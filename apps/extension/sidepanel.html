Browser behavior

Search is normal web search. Compare and Prepare are used only when you select them.

Privacy and confirmation

Page contents are read only after you press Read page. Password, one-time-code, and card-number pages are blocked from content extraction. The extension does not read browsing history, cookies, passwords, payment credentials, or hidden background tabs, and does not persist page context.

Important provider handoffs still require explicit confirmation in the browser flow.
